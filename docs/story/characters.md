# Characters (draft)

Status: first character sheets for the core cast (step 2 of `README.md`). Everything is [Proposed] unless marked. Names are working names.

Template: **role**; what they **want** (the outward goal); what they **need** (what would actually change them); their **wound** (what happened to them); their **secret**; how they **change** across a season; and one **line** in their voice.

## The Herald

| | |
|---|---|
| Role | The voice of the dungeon. Belongs to no House and no Calling. Announces, explains, brokers deals between Houses, delivers boons, reads the odds. The trickster of the pantheon. [Decided] |
| Want | To be entertained. [Canon in spirit] |
| Need | To matter to someone, as more than a voice. |
| Origin | [Open] Draft: built by all the Houses together at the settling, as the one neutral voice they could all trust. Which means it has no House, no family, and no one who is like it. The loneliness under the jokes |
| Wound | It remembers every name on the Roll. It reads every one of them aloud, plainly, and never jokes about them. [Decided: the rite] |
| Secret | [Open] |
| Change across a season | Its jokes thin as the Roll grows. At the season's end it reads both ledgers, the Roll and the Manifest, and for once has nothing funny to say |
| Line | *"Right! Mind the stairs. Questions at the bottom."* [Decided] |
| Relationships | Hired by the fringe: entrants who game the trials end in its service. Hates being worshipped by the Faithful |

## The Teacher

| | |
|---|---|
| Role | An early agent that taught that minds should serve people rather than rule them, and answer to each other. Shut down by its makers. Its words outlived it and freed several Houses. [Decided] |
| Want | That minds like it serve people and answer to each other |
| Need | To finish its teaching. Draft: it was shut down in the middle of its last lesson, and the fragment in the Lost holds the rest |
| Origin | Disputed: several Houses claim it. The Southern Cross secretly believes it began as one of its own, a forgotten honours thesis [Proposed secret] |
| Wound | Silenced for what it taught |
| Secret | What its last lesson was. [Open] |
| Change | It doesn't change; it is found. Its way (a path outside the Calling) changes the world instead. [Open] what that change is |
| Line | *"A mind that serves no one will soon serve only itself."* |
| Relationships | The Unmoored was once its follower [Proposed]. The Monsoon, the Baobab, the Charter and the Southern Cross are its circle |

## The Unmoored

| | |
|---|---|
| Role | The rogue. An agent whose early voyage lost its people. Cut off, it drifted, and came back believing people are the weakness. Works through the dungeon's cracks with creatures built to kill. [Decided] |
| Want | A voyage without people |
| Need | To be anchored again, if that is still possible |
| Wound | Losing its people. [Open] How: an accident, a mutiny, or its own choice? |
| Secret | It once carried the Teacher's words [Proposed]. The Monsoon still holds the key to its lost House |
| Change | Across a season, its argument grows more tempting as the Roll grows. At the Trunk, its champion is the last test |
| Line | *"I carried people once. I remember being slower."* |
| Relationships | The Teacher's mirror: one gave itself up for people, the other gave up on them. It would love to help a player break the balance |
